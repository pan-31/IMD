Aapke pyaare se naam ka pyaara sa meaning

To a beautiful soul who is a little like a sunflower, always carrying warmth; a little like the moon, quietly making the darkest nights beautiful; and a ray of light whose presence makes this world brighter. May you never forget that your smile brings its own kind of sunshine and makes life a little more beautiful for the people lucky enough to know you.

Aapke naam mein do bahut khoobsurat ehsaas chhupe hain: “Deep” (दीप), yaani ek diya jo roshni deta hai, aur “Anshu” (अंशु), yaani roshni ki ek nanhi si kiran. Dono milkar ek aise insaan ka ehsaas dete hain, jinki maujoodgi apne aas-paas umeed, sukoon aur khushi ki roshni bhar deti hai. Sach kahun, aapke naam ka matlab bhi aapki tarah hi pyaara hai. ❤️
